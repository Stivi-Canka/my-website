Undercurrents

Open weights are being used as a pricing weapon; analog silicon is the long-run cost floor

Open weights · Jul 27 2026

Frontier scores, published weights, custom licence

Moonshot released Kimi K3's weights on 27 July: 2.8 trillion total parameters with 104 billion active, 16 of 896 experts routed per token, 69 Kimi Delta Attention layers plus 24 Gated MLA across 93 layers, a 401M-parameter MoonViT-V2 vision encoder, and a 1,048,576-token context. The scores are not uniformly behind the closed labs: 93.5 on GPQA Diamond against Claude Opus 4.8's 91.0, 91.2 on BrowseComp against Fable 5's 88.0, but 67.5 on DeepSWE against GPT-5.6 Sol's 73.0. The weights ship under Moonshot's own "Kimi K3 License", not a permissive one — the pattern in the item below.

The commercial consequence, in Nathan Lambert's phrasing, is that strong open weights "massively reduce the margin potential for the closed labs." Two effects follow: labs have fewer profits to reinvest in the next model, and the market marks down their terminal value, which weakens the next fundraise. Hold that against section 04, where a chipmaker and a state fund — not venture firms — are the ones setting prices.

Open weights · March 2026

The same squeeze reached voice a quarter earlier

Mistral's Voxtral TTS took 68.4% against ElevenLabs Flash v2.5's 31.6% in a zero-shot custom-voice preference test, claims parity with ElevenLabs v3 — the premium, higher-latency tier — on emotion steering, produces a ten-second sample in roughly 70 ms at about 9.7× real time, and lists at $0.016 per thousand characters. ElevenLabs' business is per-character pricing for exactly that output.

The detail most coverage skipped: the weights are published under CC BY-NC 4.0. No competitor can legally self-host them for commercial service, so the pressure on ElevenLabs comes from Mistral's own hosted price while the open release does the marketing and the recruiting. That is a pricing strategy wearing an open-source licence, and together with Moonshot's custom terms it looks like the template rather than the exception.

Analog compute · Oct 2025 – Mar 2026

Memristors do the multiply-accumulate in physics

A memristor holds its weight as physical conductance, so a crossbar of them computes rather than merely stores. Drive a voltage along each row and Ohm's law performs the multiplication at every junction; Kirchhoff's current law sums the results down each column; the answer arrives as a current without the weights ever travelling to a compute unit. Since data movement rather than arithmetic consumes most of the energy and latency in GPU inference, the ceiling on this approach is discussed in orders of magnitude rather than percentages — one analog array replaces the fetch, the multiply and the accumulate in a single physical step.

Two recent results moved it toward practicality. A DGIST-led group published wafer-scale fabrication of memristive passive crossbar circuits in Nature Communications, holding roughly 95% average device yield across 4-inch wafers with CMOS-compatible processing and no high-temperature steps — manufacturing yield, not device physics, has been the blocker for a decade. And in March a bismuth-selenide crossbar (gold–Bi2Se3–titanium junctions) ran a fully analog, all-hardware reservoir-computing controller on about 7 µW, with 10–40% analog conductance tuning and under 1% retention loss over 10,000 seconds.

The distance left is the honest part. That controller balanced a lever, not a transformer; a 4-inch wafer is not 300-millimetre production; and analog precision, drift and write endurance still bound what can be served this way. But the thing it attacks is exactly the thing the $500 billion of financing in section 04 is being raised to buy.

The price of frontier compute is now equity plus research access

Nvidia took a position at both ends of the frontier this week: a financing guarantee for the largest buyer of its chips, and equity in a lab with nothing to sell. In the second deal the consideration was not only money.

TechCrunch · Jul 27 · Rebecca Bellan

Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence partners with Nvidia to scale its research

Terms Long-term partnership with an undisclosed investment — multiple billions per a source familiar with the deal, $5B per Bloomberg — giving SSI access to Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform, which is expected to raise its compute "by an order of magnitude."
The tell Nvidia, already an investor, says it signed after obtaining "rare access" into SSI's closely guarded research. A lab with no shipped product and no revenue paid partly in visibility.
Why it matters SSI's premise was a "straight shot" to superintelligence with no commercial distraction. It still needed a strategic to fund the run — the same structure as the OpenAI package, two orders of magnitude smaller.

Nvidia in talks to back OpenAI to the tune of $500B+

Structure Reportedly in talks to guarantee ~$250B of financing for OpenAI to lease a 10 GW Ohio data-centre project being developed by SoftBank, while separately discussing up to $350B to finance the chips inside it.
Why it matters Vendor financing at sovereign scale. The supplier now guarantees its customer's ability to buy from it, which moves the risk off Nvidia's income statement and onto its balance sheet — and makes the demand signal circular.
